\section*{Actionable Insights}
\begin{Form}
  \CheckBox[name=01]{} Practice self-acceptance: Embrace your strengths and limitations without self-criticism.\\
  \CheckBox[name=02]{} Focus on personal growth, not competition: Set goals based on your own progress.\\
  \CheckBox[name=03]{} Separate your tasks from others': Take responsibility only for what is truly yours.\\
  \CheckBox[name=04]{} Cultivate community feeling: Seek ways to contribute and build a sense of belonging.\\
  \CheckBox[name=05]{} Build horizontal relationships: Engage with others as equals, fostering cooperation.\\
  \CheckBox[name=06]{} Encourage rather than praise or criticize: Affirm effort and value in others.\\
  \CheckBox[name=07]{} Trust and have confidence in others: Build relationships based on mutual support.\\
  \CheckBox[name=08]{} Contribute without expectation of recognition: Find meaning in helping others.\\
  \CheckBox[name=09]{} Value ordinary life and the present moment: Appreciate everyday experiences.\\
  \CheckBox[name=10]{} Have the courage to be normal: Let go of the pursuit of easy superiority and live authentically.
\end{Form}
