\section{The Third Night – Discard Other People's Tasks}

\subsection*{Overview}
Chapter 3 centers on the concept of “separation of tasks,” a core Adlerian principle. The philosopher and youth discuss how much of our suffering comes from living to satisfy the expectations of others and seeking recognition. True freedom, the philosopher argues, is found in the courage to be disliked and in discarding the desire for approval.

\subsection*{Key Concepts and Principles}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Deny the Desire for Recognition:} The philosopher asserts that seeking recognition or trying to satisfy others’ expectations leads to a life of worry and suffering. Happiness comes from living authentically, not from being liked or validated by others.
    \item \textbf{Separation of Tasks:} This principle teaches us to distinguish between our own responsibilities and those of others. We should only concern ourselves with tasks that are truly ours, and let others handle their own. This reduces unnecessary anxiety and conflict.
    \item \textbf{Freedom is No Fear of Being Disliked:} True freedom is achieved when we are unconcerned by others’ judgments and have the courage to be disliked. This means accepting that not everyone will approve of us, and that’s okay.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Examples}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Separation of Tasks Example:} A parent wants their child to study hard, but the act of studying is the child’s task, not the parent’s. The parent should support, not control, the child’s choices.
    \item \textbf{Desire for Recognition Example:} An employee constantly seeks praise from their manager, feeling anxious when it’s not received. This dependence on external validation leads to dissatisfaction.
    \item \textbf{Freedom to Be Disliked Example:} A person chooses a career path that others disapprove of, but finds fulfillment by following their own values rather than others’ expectations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Strategies for Authentic Living}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Practice Separation of Tasks:} Regularly ask yourself, “Is this my task or someone else’s?” Focus only on what you can control.
    \item \textbf{Let Go of Recognition:} Shift your motivation from pleasing others to living in accordance with your own principles.
    \item \textbf{Accept Dislike as a Cost of Freedom:} Recognize that being disliked is sometimes the price of living authentically and freely.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Practical Applications}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Personal Growth:} Build self-reliance by focusing on your own tasks and values.
    \item \textbf{Therapy and Coaching:} Help clients identify where they are living for others’ approval and guide them toward separation of tasks and authentic choices.
\end{itemize}
