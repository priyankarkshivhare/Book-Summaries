\section*{Keywords}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{ l X }
    \textit{Intelligent Investor:} & An individual who is patient, disciplined, eager to learn, emotionally resilient, and capable of independent thought in the context of investing \\ 
    \textit{Speculator:} & An individual who seeks to profit from short-term market fluctuations, often with little regard for underlying value or fundamentals. \\
    \textit{Defensive Investor:} & An individual who focuses on avoiding serious mistakes or losses, and seeks freedom from effort, annoyance, and frequent decision-making. \\
    \textit{Enterprising Investor:} & An individual who is willing to put in the time and effort to seek out and analyze investment opportunities, often taking on more risk for potentially higher rewards. \\
    \textit{Tangible assets value:} & The intrinsic worth of physical assets, such as real estate, machinery, as well as financial balances \\
    \textit{Margin of safety:} & The principle of investing with a cushion against errors in judgment or unforeseen market downturns \\
\end{tabularx}
