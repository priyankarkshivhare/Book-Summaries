\section*{Actionable Insights}
\begin{Form}
  \CheckBox[name=01]{} Name the cue before deciding (reciprocation, consistency, social proof, liking, authority, scarcity).\\
  \CheckBox[name=02]{} Precommit written criteria for purchases/agreements; decide by standards, not cues.\\
  \CheckBox[name=03]{} Install a 24-hour pause for high-stakes buys or commitments.\\
  \CheckBox[name=04]{} Verify domain expertise; seek a second opinion for authoritative claims.\\
  \CheckBox[name=05]{} Spot extreme-then-moderate requests; evaluate the smaller ask on merits.\\
  \CheckBox[name=06]{} Check independence of reviews/ratings; prefer multiple, credible sources.\\
  \CheckBox[name=07]{} Separate person from proposal; accept only diagnostic information.\\
  \CheckBox[name=08]{} Ask why scarce; re-evaluate away from timers and limited-count screens.\\
  \CheckBox[name=09]{} Repay gifts commensurately; decline unwanted debts without guilt.\\
  \CheckBox[name=10]{} Prefer private, revocable commitments early; avoid public micro-commitments.
\end{Form}
